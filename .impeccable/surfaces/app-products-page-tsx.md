# Products (/products)

Scope: new route inside the established Signal Field world. Mode: Persuade (showcase) with Read for the facts. Audience: SASTRA students deciding to install, prospective members judging what ACE builds, recruiters/faculty. Job: show what ACE members have shipped, prove it is real, send people to install. Content truth: only shipped products with public listings; store ratings carry the date they were read; no invented projects, metrics or testimonials. User decisions (2026-11-20): list both Google Play and the App Store; no download counts; no app screenshots. Products so far: UniFy (Android and iOS app), Carpediem (website for SASTRA's cultural fest, carpediem.kuruksastra.in). A product without a real icon shows none; the fest site's favicon is the framework default, so Carpediem has no icon.

Research (2026-11-20): Awwwards SOTD listing (large media, creator line, award chips), Awwwards site page (giant centred title, hairline score stamp, site framed in an ink panel, floating details bar), Lusion projects (category line over large title), darkroom.engineering (display-scale names, sparse).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each product gets an Awwwards-style "site page" spread instead of a card in a grid: a display-scale name, a hairline proof stamp per store, and what it does set large across an inked band. Refuses the category default of a uniform grid of rounded app cards with icon, title and blurb.

OWN-WORLD: Ink and bone with one ACE Blue mark per line; 1px hairlines, square corners, Archivo 800 at width 112% for names, JetBrains Mono only for ratings and dates, label voice for captions. The features band is the page's inversion moment (foreground-on-background panel), never a gradient or device mockup chrome.

STORY: The visitor learns ACE ships real software students use, reads what UniFy does feature by feature, trusts the store ratings, installs it from Google Play or the App Store, and knows how to put their own project here.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, "Products" at display scale; right, one-line intro and mono count. Directly below the header rule, the first spread opens: the product's square icon, its name at display scale across cols 1-8, and in cols 9-12 a hairline stamp split per store (rating out of 5 with rating count) above two 56px slabs: ACE Blue for Google Play, outline for the App Store.

FORM: Product spread + inked features band (position 1 of 5 on the ordered list; screens reel dropped at the user's request). seed: none (pinned) "I need you to research the awwwards listing website for the UI Idea", inside the world the user pinned in PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments: "award-grade, not themed … The polished Awwwards tech-site standard with no metaphor world".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
