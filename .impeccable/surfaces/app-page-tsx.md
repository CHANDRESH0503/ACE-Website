# Surface: ACE landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade, and it introduces the club. Audience: current members first, then prospective SoC students. Job: understand who ACE is, see the clusters and the people, find the next event, then sign in or register. Constraints: the ACE logo and name, light and dark themes, mobile-first, SASTRA-only sign-in, no invented numbers. The hero video is removed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The leading tech club at SASTRA shows it by building a site at Awwwards standard. It refuses the template stack of a centred gradient headline, video, icon cards and team cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ink black #07080B and bone #EEEDE8 (inverted in light mode). ACE Blue, derived from the logo's #0171C3 and pushed electric, carries whole fields: the clusters section is drenched blue. Archivo variable (width and weight axes) at giant display scale. JetBrains Mono only for data: dates, times, coordinates and counts. Hairline 1px rules, square corners, no cards or glass.

STORY: ACE is SoC's own club. It runs eleven clusters and weekly contests, workshops and talks, and it is run by named students. The visitor believes it is the best place at SASTRA to get good at tech, then signs in, registers or applies.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed WebGL particle field forms the ACE "A" mark and disperses away from the cursor. "Association of Computing Engineers" is set huge across the bottom left. Mono corner data: SoC, SASTRA, Thanjavur coordinates, local time. The primary action, Sign in / View profile, sits beside Upcoming events, with a live next-event line from Supabase.

FORM: canon, the standing exit, taken in the user's words: "Award-grade, not themed". The bar is Igloo, Lusion, darkroom.engineering and Active Theory. seed:71bb0fb9ae967f46. Signature: the particle mark plus a scroll-scrubbed manifesto. Motion grammar: a counting preloader once per session, Lenis smooth scroll, masked line reveals, expo-out easing, a pinned horizontal cluster reel with ink-wipe panel inversions, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
